# Csupor Craft Beer — Website Development Brief

You are building a complete, production-quality website for **Csupor Craft Beer Sörfőzde**, a small Hungarian craft brewery and taproom located in Hatvan.

Your goal is to create a **simple, premium, visually memorable 3–5 page website** that represents the brewery authentically and makes it easy for visitors to understand what Csupor Craft Beer is, discover their beers, visit the taproom, and get in contact.

Do NOT over-engineer the website. This is a small local business, so every section must have a clear purpose.

Create a maximum of **3–5 pages**.

Do NOT automatically create pages just to reach the maximum.

Determine the smallest useful information architecture.

Purpose:

Immediately communicate what Csupor Craft Beer is and why someone should visit.

Potential content:

- strong hero
- short brewery introduction
- current beer / beer selection teaser
- taproom experience
- atmosphere / photography
- awards / credibility
- visit CTA
- location
- opening hours
- footer

The homepage should be the strongest page.

### 2. Sörök / Beers

Purpose:

Showcase the brewery's beers.

Potential content:

- current beer selection
- beer cards
- beer style
- short description

- seasonal / rotating beers
